{# General tips for creating lesson material. Used on course_form.html and builder.html #}
  • One lesson — one idea. If you need “and also…”, make a second lesson.
  • Show, don't tell. Film your hands, the screen or the tool — not only your face.
  • Start with the result. Show what “done right” looks like in the first seconds.
  • Name the common mistake and show how to fix it.
  • Good light, quiet room, landscape mode. A phone is enough.
  • Time: about 1–3 minutes per lesson works best. It's a tip, not a limit.
